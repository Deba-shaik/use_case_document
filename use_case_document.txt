# Custom GPT Use Case Document

## GPT Name

Employee Leave Assistant

## Target Users

### Primary Users

Employees who need information about company leave policies.

### Secondary Users

Managers and HR team members who need to clarify leave-related rules or
guide employees.

## Problem Statement

Employees frequently have questions about leave types, eligibility,
application procedures, approval processes, and other leave-policy
rules. Finding this information manually across HR documents can take
time and may result in repeated questions to HR.

The Employee Leave Assistant will provide conversational access to
approved leave-policy information.

## Expected Outcomes

-   Target answering common leave-policy questions within 2 minutes.
-   Target covering at least 50 common leave-policy questions.
-   Target achieving 90% correct responses during defined testing.
-   Target reducing repetitive leave-policy questions to HR by 40%.
-   Target 30 employees participating in the pilot.
